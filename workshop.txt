title=My Tailor Is Rich - Clothing & Shoe Sizes
description=[h1]My Tailor Is Rich — Clothing & Shoe Sizes[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Build 42.21 · Singleplayer & Multiplayer[/b]
description=
description=That perfect leather jacket on a zombie? It might be two sizes too small. Those army boots? Size 46, and you wear a 41.
description=
description=My Tailor Is Rich gives every piece of clothing and every pair of shoes a size. Find what fits, alter what doesn't, sew your own clothes from patterns... and think twice before sprinting in boots three sizes too big.
description=
description=[h2]At a glance[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Clothing sizes[/b] from XS to XXL, based on your character's weight.
description=[*][b]Shoe sizes[/b] from 35 to 47 (EU): watch out for blisters, trips and lost shoes.
description=[*][b]Alterations[/b]: make clothes bigger or smaller with your Tailoring skill.
description=[*][b]Wear and repair[/b]: clothes wear out, and you can recondition them.
description=[*][b]Sewing patterns[/b]: trace them from your favourite clothes or find them in stores.
description=[*][b]Sewing machines[/b]: faster and better results... but noisy.
description=[*]Works with [b]clothing mods[/b] and in [b]multiplayer[/b].
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Requirements[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3694097672]TooltipLib[/url][/b] (required): shows size and condition in item tooltips.
description=[*][b]Not compatible[/b] with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3491510356]Realistic Clothes[/url]. This mod is a full rewrite of it for Build 42.21: don't enable both.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Clothes that fit (or don't)[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Your size depends on your [b]weight[/b]: XS up to 50 kg, S up to 65, M up to 75, L under 85, XL under 100, XXL 100 and above. Gain or lose weight, and your size changes too.
description=[*]You don't know the size of the clothes you find. Right-click and choose [b]Check clothes size[/b] to read the label. With little Tailoring skill, your character can only guess.
description=[*]All the clothes on the same zombie are consistent in size.
description=[*]Hats, masks, gloves, jewelry, belts and underwear are one size fits all.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h3]Too big[/h3]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Keeps you less warm.
description=[*]Tops slow down your attacks.
description=[*]Loose pants or skirts can [b]fall down[/b] if you have no belt and both hands are busy.
description=[*]You can [b]trip[/b] when landing after a fence, even when walking (more often when running).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h3]Too small[/h3]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Takes longer to put on.
description=[*]Can [b]rip[/b] when you climb.
description=[*]You can [b]trip[/b] after climbing a wall.
description=[*]Causes muscle stiffness.
description=[*]Far too small: can't be worn at all. Put on weight, and the clothes that no longer fit come off.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Tailoring[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Alter[/b] a garment one size up (with fabric strips) or one size down (with paperclips), using a needle, thread and scissors. Your chances depend on your Tailoring skill and the fabric.
description=[*]Clothes [b]wear out[/b] with dirt, blood, rain and tight fits. [b]Recondition[/b] them with fabric strips or a spare copy of the same item.
description=[*]When you [b]craft clothes[/b], you choose their size in the crafting window.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Shoe sizes (EU 35 to 47)[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Your character has a [b]fixed shoe size[/b]: 35 to 42 for women, 39 to 47 for men. You'll find it on the character screen, next to your weight.
description=[*]Work, army and hiking boots mostly come in men's sizes; strappy and dress shoes mostly in women's. A zombie's shoes match its sex.
description=[*]The size is printed inside: [b]Check clothes size[/b] reads it, no skill needed.
description=[*]Improvised foot wraps (rags, burlap, denim, leather, tarp, twine) fit everyone. Shoes you craft are made to your size.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[table]
description=[tr][td][b]Fit[/b][/td][td][b]What happens[/b][/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]3+ sizes too small[/td][td]They won't go on[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]1–2 sizes too small[/td][td][b]Discomfort[/b], [b]blisters[/b] after walking a while (sooner when running, your character complains first), stiff feet[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Right size or 1 size bigger[/td][td]All good[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]2+ sizes too big[/td][td]You tire faster when running, occasional blisters, [b]trips[/b] after fences and walls, weaker [b]stomps[/b] on downed zombies (as weak as barefoot)[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]3+ sizes too big[/td][td]Discomfort, and you can [b]lose a shoe[/b] when running, vaulting, climbing or falling (flip-flops and slippers fly off easily, laced boots hold better)[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=
description=Blisters are small foot wounds that can't give you the zombie infection. Like any foot injury, they slow you down until they heal: bandage them!
description=
description=[h2]Sewing patterns[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Trace a pattern[/b] at a table from any garment or pair of shoes (scissors, pen, paper). The original gets cut up in the process.
description=[*]Or find [b]store-bought patterns[/b] in sewing shops, tailoring shelves and fashion bookstores. They can be for any clothes, [b]including those from other mods[/b].
description=[*][b]Sew from a pattern[/b] in the size of your choice, with the same fabric as the original (cotton, denim, leather). At low skill, you may end up one size off. Each pattern can be used 5 times.
description=[*]Sewing by hand requires a [b]thimble[/b].
description=[*]Shoes are for experts: Tailoring 8, a needle [b]and an awl[/b], glue, and leather or fabric. They're always sewn by hand. Rubber shoes (wellies, flip-flops) can't be made from a pattern.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Sewing machines[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Electric machine[/b]: sits on a table and needs power. Twice as fast, and counts as +2 Tailoring levels.
description=[*][b]Treadle machine[/b]: a heavy cabinet, no power needed. 25% faster, +1 Tailoring level.
description=[*]Look in sewing and clothing shops, supermarkets, garages, storage units, attics, sheds, closets... anywhere a sewing kit might turn up.
description=[*]Click a machine to sew from a pattern (pick the size and how many), alter or recondition clothes.
description=[*]But beware: machines are [b]noisy[/b] and attract zombies (the electric one from much further away). They also [b]wear out[/b], break needles and jam. Keep them running with a screwdriver and a bit of cooking oil (vegetable or olive).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
description=
description=Fully playable in multiplayer: everyone sees the same sizes, wear and sewing results. Only one player can use a sewing machine at a time.
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=
description=Almost everything can be tuned: skill and XP, action speed, how harsh bad fits are, clothes wear, shoe sizes, patterns, thimble, sewing machines, loot rarity... Clothes from other mods get a size automatically, and two lists let you add or exclude specific items if needed. Full details on [url=https://github.com/cyberbobjr/MyTailorIsRich]GitHub[/url].
