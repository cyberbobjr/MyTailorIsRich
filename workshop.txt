description=[b]Build 42.21 · Singleplayer & Multiplayer[/b]

description=Clothes and shoes finally have sizes. Loot that doesn't fit, resize it with your tailoring skills, and think twice before sprinting in boots three sizes too big.

description=[h2]Clothing sizes (XS to XXL)[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Your character's size follows their [b]weight[/b]: XS ≤ 50 kg, S ≤ 65, M ≤ 75, L < 85, XL < 100, XXL ≥ 100.
description=[*]A found garment's size is [b]unknown[/b]. Right-click it and choose [b]Check clothes size[/b]. Reading the label may require some Tailoring; otherwise your character only guesses.
description=[*]All clothes on the same zombie corpse have consistent sizes.
description=[*]Effects of a bad fit:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]too big[/b]: less insulation, slower combat for tops, loose pants or skirts can [b]fall down[/b] without a belt when both hands are busy, can make you [b]trip[/b] when landing after a fence, even at walking pace (less often than when running);
description=[*][b]too small[/b]: longer to put on, can [b]rip[/b] when climbing, can make you [b]trip[/b] after climbing a wall, muscle stiffness, and impossible to wear when far too small. If you gain weight, clothes that no longer fit come off.
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]Resize[/b] a garment one size up (fabric strips) or down (paperclips) with needle, scissors and thread. Success depends on Tailoring and fabric.
description=[*][b]Clothes wear out[/b] over time (dirt, blood, wetness, tight fit). [b]Recondition[/b] them with fabric strips or a spare copy of the same item.
description=[*][b]Crafted clothes[/b]: choose their size in the crafting window.
description=[*][b]Clothes from other mods are sized automatically.[/b] Known body locations use their usual profile. Any other garment (including new body locations added by mods) gets a size from what it covers: torso, legs, both, or feet for shoes. Accessories (hats, masks, gloves, jewelry, belts…), underwear and cosmetics stay unsized. Two sandbox lists handle the rest: extra body locations to size, and items to exclude.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Shoe sizes (EU 35 to 47)[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Each character has a [b]fixed foot size[/b] depending on sex: women 35–42, men 39–47. It is shown on the character screen next to your weight.
description=[*]Found shoes lean toward the usual wearer of the model. Work, army and hiking boots are mostly men's sizes; strapped and fancy shoes mostly women's. Shoes on a corpse match the zombie's sex.
description=[*]The size is printed inside the shoe: [b]Check clothes size[/b] reads it without any skill.
description=[*]Improvised foot wraps (rags, burlap, denim, leather, tarp, twine) fit anyone. Crafted shoes are made to the crafter's size.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Fit[/th][th]Effects[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td]3+ sizes too small[/td][td]Can't be worn[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]1–2 sizes too small[/td][td][b]Discomfort[/b] moodle, [b]blisters[/b] (a friction gauge fills while walking, faster when running; your character warns you halfway), foot stiffness[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Right size or 1 size bigger[/td][td]Nothing[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]2+ sizes too big[/td][td]More [b]endurance[/b] used when running, rare blisters, can make you [b]trip[/b] after a fence or a wall, weaker [b]stomps[/b] on downed zombies (down to barefoot level)[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]3+ sizes too big[/td][td]Discomfort, and you can [b]lose a shoe[/b] when running, sprinting, vaulting, climbing or falling (more often with flip-flops and slippers, less with laced boots)[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=
description=A blister is a small foot scratch without zombie infection. Like any foot injury it slows you down until it heals, so bandage it.
description=
description=[h2]Sewing patterns[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Right-click a sized garment or pair of shoes: [b]Trace a pattern[/b]. You need scissors, a pen or pencil and sheets of paper (more for complex clothes). The model must be in good condition (50% or more) and is kept intact.
description=[*]Right-click the pattern: [b]Sew from pattern[/b], then pick any size (XS–XXL, or EU 35–47 for shoes). The new item comes out with its size known.
description=[*]Materials follow the fabric of the model: cotton (ripped sheets, cotton fabric roll), denim (denim strips, denim rolls) or leather (leather strips, tanned leather). Bigger sizes use more fabric. Leather work also accepts an awl and a sharp knife.
description=[*][b]Precision[/b]: the result can come out one size off (bigger or smaller). The risk goes from 50% down to 0% with the Tailoring level of the tracer plus that of the sewer.
description=[*]A pattern survives [b]5 sewings[/b] (success or failure), then falls apart.
description=[*]Required Tailoring: garment difficulty + fabric (cotton 0, denim 1, leather 2); tracing needs one level less. [b]Shoes need level 6[/b] (sandbox option).
description=[*]No pattern for ballistic protection or items without a sewable fabric.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
description=
description=The server decides everything: sizes, resizing, wear, blisters, stiffness and lost shoes. It then syncs the result to clients. All timed actions run through the vanilla Build 42 network system.
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=
description=Tailoring level requirement, tailoring XP, action time, rip / drop / trip / stiffness multipliers, insulation and combat penalties, clothes degrading (on/off, min/max days, failure chance, protection and resistance loss), custom clothes list, [b]extra sized body locations[/b], [b]excluded clothes[/b], [b]shoe sizes on/off[/b], [b]shoe fit effects intensity[/b] (0 disables blisters, discomfort, extra endurance and lost shoes), [b]sewings per pattern[/b], [b]Tailoring level for shoe patterns[/b].
description=
description=[h2]Debug[/h2]
description=
description=In debug mode, or in MP with a role allowed to edit items, right-click a garment or shoes and open [b][Debug] Size[/b]. It shows your size, foot size, friction gauge and stomp power, lets you set any size, or clears it for a new roll.
description=
description=[h2]Good to know[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]In Build 42.21 the vanilla clothing [b]RunSpeedModifier[/b] is only shown in tooltips and never applied to movement. Only bags actually use it. This mod slows you down the way the engine allows: foot injuries (blisters) and endurance.
description=[*]Arm soreness after fights comes from [b]vanilla shoving[/b]: every push adds arm strain. Tight tops only make it last longer.
description=[/list]
