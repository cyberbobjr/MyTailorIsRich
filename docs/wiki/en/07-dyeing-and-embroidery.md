# Dyeing and embroidery

## Dyeing

Right-click a garment or shoes and pick one of the dyes you carry: **industrial dye** or **hair dye** (each option shows a color swatch).

- You also need **water**: 2, 4 or 6 L depending on the garment's size, and 0.2 to 0.75 L of dye.
- The garment is taken off if worn, takes the color of the dye and comes out **soaked**.
- No skill needed; a little Tailoring XP.
- What can be dyed: any garment whose clothing model accepts a tint, from the base game or from other mods, plus the clothes the vanilla **Dye Clothes** recipe accepts.
- Printed, camouflage or patterned clothes keep their texture colors: the option is greyed out with an explanation (shown only when you carry a dye).

In multiplayer, other players see a dyed garment once it is put on.

> Capture à faire : the Dye submenu with color swatches.

## Embroidering a name

**Embroider a name**: needle, 1 thread, thimble if required, **Tailoring 1**.

- Type up to **24 characters**. The garment is renamed (for example `Jacket "Bob"`) and its tooltip shows the embroidery.
- **Unpick the embroidery** with scissors brings the previous name back.
- Not for shoes or rigid armor.
- You can also embroider on a sewing machine (**Embroidery** tab, see [Sewing machines](06-sewing-machines.md)); unpicking is always done by hand.

> Capture à faire : the embroidery text box and the renamed garment's tooltip.

Both features can be turned off in the [sandbox options](08-sandbox-options.md).
