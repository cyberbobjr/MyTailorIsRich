# Alterations and wear

## Resizing a garment

Right-click a garment whose size you know: **Resize clothes**, then **Upsize clothes** (one size up, "let out") or **Downsize clothes** (one size down, "take in"). You can also do it on a sewing machine (**Alteration** tab, see [Sewing machines](06-sewing-machines.md)).

| | Let out (bigger) | Take in (smaller) |
|---|---|---|
| Tools | Needle, scissors, thread | Needle, scissors, thread |
| Material | Fabric strips of the garment's fabric (ripped sheets, denim strips, leather strips) | Paperclips |
| Required Tailoring | difficulty + fabric + 1 | difficulty + fabric |

- Garment difficulty goes from 1 (T-shirt, pants) to 3 (dress, boiler suit, bulky jacket); fabric adds 0 (cotton), 1 (denim) or 2 (leather).
- Success is 50% at the required level, +25% per level above.
- A garment can be resized **once**. About one found garment in ten has already been altered.
- A failed alteration can wear the garment out.
- By hand you also need a **thimble** (sandbox option).

## Clothes wear out

Worn clothes slowly lose condition: dirt, blood, wetness and a tight fit make it faster; Maintenance and Tailoring slow it down. As condition drops, the garment loses protection (scratch, bite, bullet) and resistance (wind, water). The tooltip of a worn garment estimates its wear. Shoes don't wear out this way.

Vanilla patches no longer change the condition of a garment that wears out with this mod.

## Reconditioning

Right-click a worn garment: **Recondition**, then **Use …** with either:

- **fabric strips** of its fabric, or
- **a spare copy** of the same item (not worn, not in your hands), which is used up and gives a better result.

You also need needle, scissors and thread (and a thimble by hand). The result depends on Tailoring and Maintenance; every repair makes the next one less effective. A failure can wear the garment further.

## Crafted clothes

When a crafting recipe makes a garment, the crafting window shows a **size button** on the output: click it to cycle through XS to XXL (it starts at your own size). The garment comes out with that size, already known. If no size was chosen, the garment comes out with an **unchosen size** and can't be worn: right-click it and use **Choose clothes size**. Crafted shoes are made to the crafter's size.
