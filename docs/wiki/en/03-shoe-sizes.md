# Shoe sizes

## Your foot size

Each character has a **fixed foot size** (EU), depending on sex: women 35 to 42, men 39 to 47. It is shown on the character screen next to your clothing size.

## Shoes you find

- The size is printed inside the shoe: **Check clothes size** reads it **without any skill**.
- Shoes lean toward the usual wearer of the model: work, army and hiking boots are mostly men's sizes; strapped and fancy shoes mostly women's.
- Shoes on a corpse match the zombie's sex.
- Improvised foot wraps (rags, burlap, denim, leather, tarp, twine) have no size and fit anyone.
- Crafted shoes are made to the crafter's size.

## Fit effects

| Fit | Effects |
|---|---|
| 3+ sizes too small | Can't be worn |
| 1–2 sizes too small | **Discomfort** moodle, **blisters**, foot stiffness |
| Right size or 1 size bigger | Nothing |
| 2+ sizes too big | More **endurance** used when running, rare blisters, can make you **trip** after a fence or a wall, weaker **stomps** on downed zombies (down to barefoot level), can **come off** when vaulting, climbing or falling |
| 3+ sizes too big | Also discomfort, and the shoe can come off when running or sprinting |

Flip-flops and slippers come off more easily, laced boots less. When a shoe almost came off, your character says so.

## Blisters

A friction gauge fills while you walk in tight shoes, faster when you run; your character warns you halfway. When it is full you get a **blister**: a small foot scratch without zombie infection. Like any foot injury it slows you down until it heals, so bandage it. At rest, the gauge empties in about one in-game hour.

> Capture à faire : the warning line and a blister on the foot in the health panel.

## Why no speed penalty?

In Build 42.21 the vanilla clothing `RunSpeedModifier` is only shown in tooltips and never applied to movement (only bags use it). The mod slows you down the way the engine allows: foot injuries and endurance.

Sandbox options: **shoe sizes on/off** and **shoe fit effects intensity** (0 disables blisters, discomfort, extra endurance and lost shoes). See [Sandbox options](08-sandbox-options.md).
