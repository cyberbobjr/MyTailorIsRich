# Sewing machines

## Two machines

- **Electric sewing machine**: sits on any table, needs **power** (the grid indoors, or a generator).
- **Treadle sewing machine**: a heavy cast-iron cabinet that stands on its own, no power needed. It also counts as a work table for tracing patterns.

Where to find them: sewing workshops and fabric shops, clothing and department stores, the bedding aisle of supermarkets, people's storage (garages, storage units, attics, sheds, storage rooms, closets) and, more rarely, wherever a sewing kit can turn up. The treadle machine also turns up among antiques. Pick them up and place them like any furniture.

## The machine panel

Click a machine (or use its right-click option) to open its panel. Only one player can use a machine at a time.

![Electric machine, Pattern tab](../../images/screenshots/shot4.png)

The top shows the machine's bonuses (and whether it is powered), its **condition** and the **Maintain** button. Then four tabs:

- **Pattern**: drag a clothing pattern into the slot (or click the slot to choose one, including patterns stored in a binder), pick the size and the **quantity** to sew in a row. The panel shows the garment that will come out, the chance of success, the risk of a size off, and the exact list of items used. It remembers the last pattern you used. Shoe patterns are sewn by hand.
- **Alteration**: let out or take in a garment.
- **Reconditioning**: repair a worn garment with fabric strips or a spare one.
- **Embroidery**: drag a garment, type the text (your first name to start with, up to 24 characters) and check the name it will get. Hidden when embroidery is disabled.

The button shows the progress ("Sewing 2/5… 45%"). Your character walks to the machine and works facing it.

## Bonuses

| | Electric | Treadle |
|---|---|---|
| Power | Required | No |
| Speed | 50% less time | 25% less time |
| Tailoring levels | +2 | +1 |
| Size precision | +4 | +2 |
| Thread | 30% less | 15% less |
| Thimble | Not needed | Not needed |
| Embroidery | Tailoring 1 (bonus included), twice as fast as by hand | Tailoring 3 (bonus not counted: straight stitch only), 25% faster |

The **sewing machine bonus** sandbox option scales all bonuses.

## Noise

A running machine can be heard by zombies, the electric one much further than the treadle one. Nearby players hear it too.

## Maintenance

- Machines wear out with use. Below **50%** they are less reliable and sometimes **break the needle** you hold; at **0%** they **jam**.
- **Maintain** with a screwdriver and a little cooking oil (vegetable or olive). The repair skill is **Electrical** for the electric machine, **Mechanics** for the treadle one; higher skill restores more condition.
- The machine keeps its wear when picked up and placed again.

> Capture à faire : a jammed machine at 0% and the Maintain needs.
