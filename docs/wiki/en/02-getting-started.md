# Getting started

## Your size

Your character's clothing size follows their **weight**:

| Size | Weight |
|---|---|
| XS | 50 kg or less |
| S | up to 65 kg |
| M | up to 75 kg |
| L | under 85 kg |
| XL | under 100 kg |
| XXL | 100 kg or more |

It changes with your weight. The character screen (Info tab) shows it next to your weight, with your foot size, for example `(L, 43)`. A mod that hides your weight (Immersive Weighing) hides it too.

> Capture à faire : the character Info tab with the size and foot size next to the weight.

## Which clothes have a size

Tops, bottoms, dresses and boiler suits have a size, whether they come from the base game or from another mod. Accessories (hats, masks, gloves, jewelry, belts, scarves, socks…), underwear and cosmetics don't. Shoes have their own EU size: see [Shoe sizes](03-shoe-sizes.md).

## Checking a size

A garment you find has an **unknown** size. Right-click it and choose **Check clothes size**:

- With enough Tailoring (the garment's difficulty minus one), your character reads the label and says the exact size.
- Otherwise they only guess: "a bit tight", "very loose"…
- Clothes in a corpse, a piece of furniture or a vehicle within reach are checked **in place**, without taking them. Clothes on the floor are picked up first.

On a corpse, the submenu offers **Selected clothes**, **All clothes on this corpse** and **All clothes on nearby corpses** (shoes included). Group checks skip known sizes and hints you can't improve at your current level.

![Check clothes size on a corpse](../../images/screenshots/shot6.png)

A known size is added to the item's name in the inventory and shown in its tooltip, with how it fits you:

![Size in the tooltip](../../images/screenshots/shot1.png)

Clothes on the same zombie are close in size: same size most of the time, otherwise one size smaller or larger.

## Bad fit

| Fit | Effects |
|---|---|
| Too big | Less insulation; tops slow down your attacks; loose pants or skirts can **fall down** without a belt when both hands are busy; you can **trip** when landing after a fence, even at walking pace. |
| Too small | Longer to put on; can **rip** when climbing; can make you **trip** after climbing a wall; muscle stiffness in the covered areas, worse when exercising. |
| 3 sizes or more too small | Can't be put on. |

If you gain weight, clothes that no longer fit come off. Your character says why.
