## Titre

My Tailor Is Rich — clothing & shoe sizes for Project Zomboid (Build 42)

## Texte

My new Project Zomboid mod is out on the Steam Workshop! 🧵

That perfect leather jacket on a zombie? It might be two sizes too small. Those army boots? Size 46, and you wear a 41.

**My Tailor Is Rich** gives every piece of clothing and every pair of shoes a size:

- **Clothing sizes** from XS to XXL, based on your character's weight
- **Shoe sizes** from 35 to 47: watch out for blisters, trips and lost shoes
- **Alterations**, wear and reconditioning with your Tailoring skill
- **Sewing patterns**: trace them from your favourite clothes or find them in stores
- **Electric and treadle sewing machines**: faster and better... but noisy
- Works with clothing mods, in singleplayer and **multiplayer**, in 8 languages

👉 Subscribe on the Steam Workshop: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3810122988

The mod is free and open source ([GitHub](https://github.com/cyberbobjr/MyTailorIsRich)). If you enjoy it, a coffee here helps me keep adding features and translations. Thank you! ☕
